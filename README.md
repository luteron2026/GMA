# Grand Mustang Auto 🐎

A free, fan-made parody open-world browser game set at The Master's University in Placerita Canyon (Santa Clarita, CA).
It's a GTA-style game played backwards: instead of committing crimes, you do good deeds. Good deeds raise your **Divine Approval** ★, and the more good you do, the more imps come after you.

> Fan-made parody. Not affiliated with or endorsed by The Master's University or The Babylon Bee. All characters are fictional.

## Play

When GitHub Pages is on, the game is live at **https://luteron2026.github.io/GMA/** (the URL follows the repo name, so it changes if you rename the repo).

Runs in any modern browser on desktop and phone. There's nothing to install and no account.

### Controls

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Phone |
|---|---|---|
| Walk / drive | `W` `A` `S` `D` or arrow keys | Left joystick |
| Throw a Bible (on foot) · Hallelujah Horn (driving) | `Space` or click | 📖 |
| Talk, help, borrow a car, get out, kick stunned imps | `E` | ✋ |
| Go into a building (blue ring at the door) · come back out | `E` | ✋ |
| Sprint · handbrake | `Shift` | ⚡ |
| Camera distance | `C` | 🎥 |
| Chopper: climb · descend | `Space` · `Shift` (W/S fly, A/D turn) | 📖 · ⚡ |
| Forklift: raise/lower forks · Tank: fire | `Space` | 📖 |
| Pickleball: slide · serve/smash · quit | `A` `D` · `Space` (tap it right before the ball reaches you to smash) · `E` | stick · 📖 · ✋ |
| Beach volleyball: run · hit · aim · quit | `WASD` (run to the ring) · `Space` as it comes down (near the net on a high set it jumps and spikes) · `A`/`D` pick the line, `W` send it over, `S` dink · `E` | stick · 📖 · ✋ |
| Soccer: run · shoot · pass · quit | `WASD` · hold and release `Space` to shoot (`A`/`D` aim), `Space` to tackle · `Shift` pass or call for it (hold to sprint) · `E` | stick · 📖 · ⚡ · ✋ |
| Put every vehicle back where it started | 🔄 button, or **Reset vehicles** in the pause menu | 🔄 |
| Sound · pause | `M` · `Esc` | 🔊 · ⏸ |

## What's in it

- **The real campus, at real scale:** The map is built from OpenStreetMap data, so buildings have their real outlines and sit where they really are. Streets, parking lots and fields follow the real layout too. That includes the MacArthur Center (where chapel meets in Bross Court), the Dunkin Student Center / Mustang Grill, Powell Library, the Reese Center, King Hall, the six dorms (Hotchkiss, Dixon, Slight, Sweazy, Waldock, C.W. Smith), North Campus (Biblical Studies, Music, Business, Humanities), Lou Herwaldt Stadium / Reese Field with its baseball diamond, the intramural field, Placerita Bible Church, the Placeritos neighborhood, Placerita Creek, and the trails up the hill behind campus.
- **Real hills, trees and roofs from LiDAR:** The ground is the real ground, from a 2023 USGS airborne LiDAR survey (about 17 laser points per square meter). The ridge behind campus rises about 50 m, Prayer Hill sits on the real hilltop, and the cut slopes, terraces and creek channel are where they really are. Roads climb, so cars slow down uphill and pitch and roll with the slope, and walking uphill is slower. All ~2,000 trees stand where the LiDAR found them, at their measured height and crown width, sorted into broadleaf trees, palms and conifers. Chaparral and hedges grow where the survey found low brush. Each roof's height and shape (pitched or flat, single or multi-level) is fitted to the points that hit it. Carports and sheds the map doesn't have show up too. The mountains around the canyon come from USGS elevation data.
- **What it looks like, from Street View notes:** Buildings have the real wall and roof colors, materials and window styles, written down from Google Street View (descriptions only, no images copied). The campus style is tan stucco, brown shingle roofs and dark-brown trim, with stacked-stone piers and bases, timber gable entrances (Education, Powell Library), the Mustang Grill's deck on the Dunkin Student Center (from photos of it: a tall timber gable pavilion with an open king-post truss on stacked-stone piers that run from the lower patio up past the deck, lantern sconces on the piers, a pergola deck running east along the building, a deep brown fascia, wood-post railings with black picket panels, patio heaters and slatted tables up top, a warm-lit cafe patio underneath, and stone lantern bollards among ornamental grasses, red yucca and boulders), the church bell tower and spire, the chapel's long stucco stair, Biblical Studies' brick columns and railed walkway, the round fountain plaza in front of Rutherford (placed on the aerial photo: a stacked-stone basin with a pale cap, a stone pedestal and a spilling bowl, a terracotta paving ring, wooden benches and the flagpole with the American flag), the Hotchkiss pool (measured on the aerial photo: its deck runs from the pool house at the west end to a white corrugated-metal shade canopy with cafe tables along the Placerita side, with a fence, lounge chairs and a lifeguard chair), the octagonal Oaks Pavilion gazebo on the Under the Oaks lawn and the stone entrance monument. Doors are on the real entrance sides. The streets have their real center lines, shoulders, crosswalks, speed bumps (cars bounce over them), red curbs, wooden utility poles with wires and streetlights, stop/dead-end/bump/pedestrian-beacon/do-not-enter signs, painted STOP and BUMP, the block walls, rail and ranch fences, brick-and-iron fences, stone gate pillars with lanterns, directory signs, a row of blue and white flagpoles along Placerita by the stadium, palms, stone pines, crape myrtles and flower beds. Houses take the colors typical of their street.
- **Street names:** Green street signs stand at intersections (Placerita Canyon Rd, Placeritos Blvd, Meadview Ave, Quigley Canyon Rd, Amelia Ln). The HUD shows the name of the street you're on, or else the area you're in.
- **Names from the university's own campus map:** Buildings OpenStreetMap leaves unnamed (IT Administration Center, Wismer Technology Center, Disability Services, Sport Management Center, Plant Operations, the Soccer & Baseball Clubhouse) and the Kinesiology Center (a building only the LiDAR has) carry their names from the university's interactive campus map, whose markers have coordinates. So do the lawns (Louis Herwaldt, North Campus, RCSM, Swixon), the Oaks Pavilion under the oaks, and the parking lots (Visitor's Lot P1, Residence P1, P2 along Reese Field, Trophy Coffee P2, Reese Center, North Campus, Intramural Field).
- **From the campus map's photos:** The university's map also has about 200 photos of its buildings, and the looks were refined from them (written notes again, no images copied): the dorms' dark brown window bays running floor to floor with a window AC unit under each window (Hotchkiss, Dixon, Sweazy), Dixon's and Sweazy's lap siding, Waldock's gray siding with its brown floor bands, Slight's balcony walkway, the board-and-batten Chancellor's House, the sage siding at Vider, the cream ranch houses (Events & Production, the Oaks Lounge with its stone base), King Hall's river-rock base, the modular offices' panel siding, the music building's brick pilasters and railed walkway, the pool's white corrugated shade canopy, the Reese Center's row of tall stucco arches standing out from both long sides over its second-floor walkway, and the Business Center's stacked-stone piers, black-railed upper walkway and navy sign.

Walk out through the doors onto the deck: patio heaters, slatted tables, the timber roof on stone piers, and the view over Placerita Canyon.

Order a cold brew there too.
- **The guard shack:** Campus Safety's tan board-and-batten booth at the entrance drive off Placerita, placed from the campus map's marker and the aerial photo, with its hip roof, sliding windows, AC unit, yellow-striped curb, the officer at the window (say hi) and the white Campus Safety golf cart.
- **Made up for the game:** The interiors' floor plans (the photos show what's inside, not how the rooms fit together, so sizes and layouts are approximate), Prayer Hill's clearing and its rocks (the hill and its white cross are real: the knob above the Chancellor's House, where the trail crosses it, as seen from the main entrance), the stands behind home plate (squared up with the parking behind them), and the looks of buildings the Street View notes couldn't see (trees hide many dorms), which use the campus style.
- **Good deeds:** Students with a **!** need help (dropped textbooks, lost lanyards, ring-by-spring plans). Students with a 🚗 want a ride across campus.
- **Paved like the real thing:** Streets, drives, parking lots, walkways, the double yellow line, crosswalks, stall lines and the helipad are real geometry laid exactly on the LiDAR ground, so edges stay sharp up close. The concrete walks and plazas are traced from the USDA aerial photo (cross-checked with the LiDAR so roofs and tree crowns aren't mistaken for concrete), and every entrance has a short walk to the nearest one. Under the oaks, where the photo can't see the ground, walks are missing. Speed bumps are striped humps that follow the road. Solar panel carports shade the parking rows in the North Campus lot and along the dorm drive in front of C.W. Smith and Waldock, like the real ones (you can drive and park under them). Nose-in parking rows line both sides of the dorm drive, in front of C.W. Smith and Waldock and across from them by Sweazy and Dixon. Angled parking lines both sides of the one-way drive east of the MacArthur Center and its top by the Kinesiology Center, and runs herringbone along the drive south of Reese Field. OpenStreetMap doesn't have these rows, so they're placed where the LiDAR caught parked cars and the painted stall lines (in the laser intensity).
- **Borrowing cars:** Golf carts, commuter sedans, the facilities truck, the outreach van, and Dean Placerita's golden cart. You get them by asking nicely.
- **The tank, the chopper and the forklift:** An ROTC-surplus tank is parked on the North Campus lawn. Its tracks turn in place, nothing shoves it around, and the horn button fires a Hallelujah Shell (blanks). The Prayer Chopper sits on its own concrete pad on the lawn beside the Reese Center lot and really flies: climb, cruise over campus, and set it down anywhere, rooftops included, to get out (from a roof you climb down the ladder). The Trophy Coffee forklift steers with its back wheels and lifts a pallet of coffee sacks. The 🔄 button puts every vehicle back where it started.
- **Trophy Coffee:** The coffee shop at the south end of the MacArthur Center, as in the campus map's photos: a deep navy front with TROPHY in big white letters under a little gold star, black steel doors and windows looking into the white cafe, a navy-and-white striped awning, gooseneck lamps over the sign, and a patio with navy market umbrellas, marble bistro tables with blue-and-white rattan chairs, picnic tables and stacked-stone piers carrying a timber pergola. Order a cold brew to top up your joy, outside or at the counter inside.
- **The Dixon fireplace:** An outdoor stacked-stone fireplace with a crackling fire and smoke curling out of its chimney, in the courtyard by Dixon's fountain, with Adirondack chairs around it. Press E there to warm up (joy +40).
- **Pickleball and sand volleyball:** Four fenced pickleball courts at West Campus, with tall light poles in the fence line. Walk in through the gate and press E to play a campus regular, Pong style: slide side to side along your baseline, the ball comes off your paddle at an angle that depends on where it hits, bounces off the sidelines, and gets faster with every return. First to 7, win by 2. Two sand volleyball courts with nets sit next to them: walk onto the sand and press E for two-on-two beach volleyball with a teammate. Get to the ring (it shows where to stand to play the ball, and you're pulled the last step or two onto it) and press Space as the ball comes down: the press holds for a second, so you don't need perfect timing; the receiver passes, the partner sets and the receiver attacks, and near the net on a high set Space jumps and spikes. Steer hits with A/D, W and S. Rally scoring, first to 11, win by 2.
- **Soccer:** The Intramural Sports Field has goals with nets, corner flags and painted lines. Walk onto it and press E for a four-a-side game (three outfield players and a keeper a side). Run with the ball to dribble, hold and release Space to shoot (A/D aims), Shift to pass to the open teammate or call for the ball, Space to tackle. Keepers make saves and parry shots, and the ball goes out for throw-ins, corners and goal kicks. First to 3 goals, or whoever leads after 5 minutes.
- **From the air:** Green yards and backyard pools in the neighborhoods, and gray-green chaparral on the hills, like the aerial photos.
- **Imps:** They show up as your Divine Approval rises. Throw Bibles to stun them, then hit them again, kick them, or drive into them. Your car's Hallelujah Horn stuns every imp nearby.
- **8 missions:**
  1. **The 9:10 Sprint:** run from Waldock Hall to chapel at the MacArthur Center before the doors close.
  2. **Apple Pie Night:** carry pies from the Mustang Grill to the Hotchyard. Every bump costs a pie.
  3. **Classy Off Campus:** find a tie (Business Center), a blazer (Schaffer Center), and dress shoes (Placeritos Blvd), then get to class at the Reese Center.
  4. **Commuter Carpool:** pick up stranded commuters in the West, North, and C.W. Smith lots and drive them to chapel.
  5. **Gratitouille:** gather the ingredients for Dixon's Italian Thanksgiving.
  6. **Stadium Cleanse:** clear the imps out of Lou Herwaldt Stadium.
  7. **Quiet in the Library:** catch and shush the loud students on the King Hall lawn.
  8. **Showdown on Prayer Hill:** the final boss. Unlocks after 5 other missions.
- **Saves automatically.** Chapel Credits, finished missions, stats, your spot on campus, the car you're driving, the time of day, and your stars are all kept on your device. The game saves every few seconds and whenever you close or leave the page. Coming back shows **Continue**. A mission you were in the middle of starts over. Saves stay in that browser on that device, so private browsing or clearing site data erases them. Saves from before the real-campus map keep their credits and missions, but start you back at the spawn point.

## Publish it (free, with GitHub Pages)

1. Merge the pull request into `main`.
2. In the repo, open **Settings → Pages** and set **Source** to **GitHub Actions**.
3. The workflow in `.github/workflows/pages.yml` deploys automatically on every push to `main`. The URL shows in **Settings → Pages** and on the workflow run.

On the free plan, GitHub Pages only works for **public** repositories.

## Run it locally

The game is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript modules, with no build step. Serve the folder with any static server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000.

### Changing the map

`src/data/campus.js` is generated. Coordinates are in meters at real scale, with x pointing east and y pointing north. To change the map:

1. To pick up newer map data, export the area again at [openstreetmap.org](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) (zoom to the campus, then **Export**) and save it over `tools/data/tmu.osm`.
2. To rename a building or move a mission spot, edit the `NAMED` table or the `places` block in `tools/osm_to_campus.py`.
3. Regenerate:

```bash
pip install shapely
```

```bash
python3 tools/osm_to_campus.py tools/data/tmu.osm src/data/campus.js
```

4. Rebuild the terrain, trees and roofs (roofs are matched to building ids, so do this after step 3). The LiDAR download is about 12 million points and isn't kept in the repo:

```bash
pip install 'laspy[lazrs]' numpy scipy pillow
```

```bash
python3 tools/fetch_lidar.py /tmp/lidar.npz
```

```bash
python3 tools/lidar_to_terrain.py /tmp/lidar.npz src/data/campus.js src/data/terrain.js
```

5. Retrace the walks and plazas from the aerial photo (uses the LiDAR download from step 4 to tell concrete from roofs and tree crowns):

```bash
pip install numpy opencv-python-headless scikit-image shapely pillow
```

```bash
mkdir -p /tmp/walks && cp /tmp/lidar.npz /tmp/walks/all.npz
```

```bash
node -e "import('./src/data/campus.js').then(({CAMPUS})=>require('fs').writeFileSync('/tmp/walks/campus.json',JSON.stringify(CAMPUS)))"
```

```bash
python3 tools/naip_walks.py /tmp/walks src/data/walks.js
```
